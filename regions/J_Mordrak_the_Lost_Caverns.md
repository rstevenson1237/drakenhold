# `J Mordrak, the Lost Caverns`

**Classification:** WILD · **Difficulty Die:** d8 · **Tags:** Mythic Underworld, Vast and Quiet, Where the Wyrms Went

*Region file. Companion to the Drakenhold Setting Outline and the Setting Relational Diagram. Tables are authored at the location-stub pass (procedure step 7); location stubs, outlines and the region relational diagram follow in the engineer phase.*

---


**Overview:** WILD by scale rather than by safety. A single great tunnel runs the length of the mountain beneath F, G and H, and off it open six or seven discrete territories of wildly different character — flooded galleries gone to ooze, exhausted dwarven workings, natural cavern, the volcanic vent system that still feeds the forges, the vein the artifact was found in, and the deep dark where the wyrms went when they were driven down. A party can travel it for watches at a time in eerie, mysterious quiet. The danger is concentrated in specific places and specific triggers, and those triggers fire precisely when a party has decided the caverns are harmless. Light is a declaration here and is answered: an open flame below the Under Levels draws what is nearest, and the Referee should let a party learn that from the aftermath of someone else's torch before it learns it from its own. Every peak has both a marked descent and at least one hidden one.

**Ambiance:** Enormous quiet. The great tunnel runs beyond the reach of any light a party carries, and the sound of footsteps goes away and does not come back. Water somewhere. Warm air from one direction and cold from another. This is the mythic underworld and it answers to nothing above it.

**Layout:** A single great tunnel running the length of the mountain beneath F, G and H, with six or seven discrete territories opening off it — flooded galleries gone to ooze, exhausted dwarven workings, natural cavern, the volcanic vent system that still feeds the forges, the vein the artifact was found in, and the deep dark where the wyrms went when they were driven down. Every peak has both a marked descent and at least one hidden one. WILD by scale rather than by safety.

**Features:** Travel itself, for watches at a time in eerie quiet. The vein, which is where the artifact came out of. The vent system, which connects to FB's chimney and to FD's channels and is the mechanism behind every restoration in Peak 1. The territories, each with its own character and its own rules.

**Dangers:** Concentrated in specific places and specific triggers, and those triggers fire precisely when a party has decided the caverns are harmless. Light is a declaration here and is answered — an open flame below the Under Levels draws what is nearest, and the Referee should let a party learn that from the aftermath of someone else's torch before it learns it from its own.

**Creatures:** Lesser Wyrms in the deep tunnels, hunting by vibration and never truly alone. Elder Wyrms in the deep dark, who remember the yoke and remember it breaking and are still angry about both. Oozes in the flooded galleries. Nothing that can be bargained with except the Elder Wyrms, and only by someone who knows what happened in GB.

**Secrets:** Where the vein runs and what else is in it. That the wyrms below and the Drakmorith above are the same story from two ends. The hidden descents, one per peak at least, which are the module's answer to any gate a party cannot open.

**Treasure:** Mined gems and precious metals in the workings, in quantity, heavy. Whatever the dwarves left when they stopped digging in a hurry.

## TABLES

**Encounter** — d6, rolled on each failed Difficulty roll, resolving in the next watch. **Rolled every watch instead, and arriving rather than signalling, whenever open flame is carried below the Under Levels** — see the light rule in `I_AND_J_BLOCK.md`. Ascending.

| d6 | Encounter |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Quiet.** Watches of it. The tunnel runs beyond any light the party carries, footsteps go away and do not come back, and nothing at all happens. This result is not a wasted roll. It is the region establishing the thing the other five results violate. |
| 2 | **Warm from one side, cold from the other.** A draught the party can follow, and following it means something — the vents one way, the deep dark the other, and the difference is discoverable by anyone paying attention to their own skin. |
| 3 | **Vibration.** Something moving through rock at a distance, felt through the palms rather than heard. Lesser Wyrms hunt this way and they are never truly alone in a tunnel. |
| 4 | **Water.** A flooded stretch, a fall in the dark, or standing water that was not standing on the way in. What lives in it is slow and does not need to be fast. |
| 5 | **Answered.** Whatever was nearest has come to the light. This is the light rule arriving in person and it should never be the first time the party has heard of it. |
| 6 | **The deep dark takes an interest.** Something enormous, blind, unhurried and old enough to remember the collar. It is not hunting. It has noticed, and it can be spoken to by anyone who knows what was done at `GB`. |

## LOCATION STUBS

*Procedure step 7. Block-level material — the great tunnel, the seven territories, the descents and the light rule — lives in `I_AND_J_BLOCK.md`.*

*50 rooms budgeted across seven territories; **24 are stubbed** and the balance is unnamed fill. **Here the fill is the region's point**: watches of enormous quiet with nothing in them at all, and a Referee who fills that silence has misread the whole thing.*

**The tunnel**

### `J.1 The Great Tunnel` — The Length of the Mountain, Beyond Any Light Carried, Enormous Quiet
*Working note: the spine. Travel is measured in watches rather than rooms. Warm air from one direction and cold from another, water somewhere, and the sound of footsteps going away without coming back. **This is the mythic underworld and it answers to nothing above it.***

### `J.2 The Marked Descents` — One Per Peak, Cut and Signed, Warnings That Are Specific
*Working note: `FB.14`, `GB`'s way down, `HB.19`. Dwarven work, honestly signed, and the warnings are old, specific and not about what a party expects.*

### `J.3 The Hidden Descents` — Khorven, the Slag Channel, the Broken Floor
*Working note: `FB.4`'s chimney, `FD.16`, and `GB.20`. **The last of these was not cut by dwarves.** Every gate has an answer that is not the gate, and this is the shelf those answers sit on.*

### `J.4 The Sealed Shafts` — Warning Inscription Rather Than Stone, Nine Hundred Years Old, Still Holding
*Working note: from the Waking of the Deep. They sealed with words instead of rock, which is either confidence or a different kind of engineering, and reading them is the region's script reward.*

### `J.5 The Middens` — Wyrm Bone Where They Fell, Left Deliberately, A Statement
*Working note: nine centuries of the dwarves' side of the war, stacked where it happened and never cleared. It was left as a message and the audience was not human.*

### `J.6 The Burnt-Out Torch` — A Party That Learned It the Hard Way, Everything Legible, Recent
*Working note: **the teaching site for the light rule.** Four dead, a spent torch, and the whole sequence readable in the arrangement. If the party has already learned it themselves, this becomes people they might have warned.*

**The flooded galleries**

### `J.7 The Flooded Galleries` — Dwarven Cut Gone Under, Slow Water, Something In It
*Working note: workings the water took. Oozes, and the slow certainty of things that do not need to hurry.*

### `J.8 The Drowned Working` — Tools and Ore Still Down There, Visible, Not Reachable Cheaply
*Working note: real wealth under water, and the cost of getting it is the whole content of the location.*

### `J.9 The Outfall` — Where It Goes, Confirmed at D.6, The Water Leaves the Mountain
*Working note: closes the drain thread from `B.4a`. It is also, for a party thinking sideways, an exit.*

**The exhausted workings**

### `J.10 The Exhausted Workings` — Dug Out, Abandoned in Order, Nothing Left Worth Digging
*Working note: the dwarves finished here properly, which is a contrast the region uses.*

### `J.11 Where They Stopped in a Hurry` — Tools Down, Ore Left, Not Finished Properly
*Working note: and here they did not. Whatever moved them out did it fast, and the date is legible from what was abandoned.*

### `J.12 The Gem Faces` — Mined Wealth, In Quantity, Heavy
*Working note: the region's honest treasure and its honest problem. It is enormous, it is portable in principle, and the way out is watches of dark tunnel.*

**The natural cavern and the vents**

### `J.13 The Natural Cavern` — Nobody Cut This, Vast, Older Than Everything
*Working note: the one territory with no dwarven work in it at all, and it is the largest.*

### `J.14 The Vent System` — Feeds the Chimney and the Channels, Walkable, Hot
*Working note: **the mechanism behind every restoration in Peak 1** — `FB.4`, `FD.3`, `FD.10`. It is walkable, and walking it is how a party discovers on its own that the vents are one system. **The chain from Peak 1's prize to Peak 2's crater runs through this territory.** Light means nothing here; heat and light are indistinguishable to what lives in it.*

### `J.15 The Hot Runs` — Where It Is Not Survivable, Marked by the Dwarves, Marked Honestly
*Working note: the dwarves mapped the vents for safety and the marks still hold. Following them works.*

### `J.16 The Draught Reading` — Warm From One Side, Cold From the Other, Navigable by Skin
*Working note: the region's navigation method, available to anyone in total darkness. **This is how a party moves through `J` without light**, and it is the answer to the light rule rather than a defiance of it.*

**The vein**

### `J.17 The Vein` — Where the Artifact Came Out, Worked Out, Still Marked
*Working note: the seam it was found in, at the deepest reach of the expansion, and the workings around it are marked in the Runemasters' own hand rather than the miners'.*

### `J.18 What Was Brought Back` — The Seventh Piece, Returned to the Vein, Carried by Somebody Who Did Not Get Out
*Working note: **the last of the seven.** The third trailing entry on the register at `HE.10` — a Runemaster who took a piece *down* rather than out, all the way back to the seam it came from, and stopped there. He is still there. **The register names him and does not say where he went**, which means the count closes only for a party that reads `HE.10` and then thinks about where a Runemaster would consider a piece safest.*

### `J.19 What Else Is In It` — The Vein Runs Further, Nobody Followed It, The Reason Is Written Down
*Working note: the expansion stopped short and it was a decision rather than an exhaustion. Whoever made it wrote down why, in the Runemaster hand, at the face.*

**The deep dark**

### `J.20 The Deep Dark` — Where the Wyrms Went, Driven Down, Still Angry
*Working note: the bottom of the module. Elder Wyrms who remember the yoke and remember it breaking. Blind, so the light rule does not apply and a party that has managed torches for the whole region will find that disorienting.*

### `J.21 The Ones Who Remember the Collar` — Can Be Bargained With, Only By Someone Who Knows, Nothing Else Down Here Can
*Working note: **the only negotiation available below the Under Levels**, and the price of entry is knowing what was done in `GB`'s deep cells. `GB.19`, `HE.6` and this location are the same story from three ends, and a party that has all three has something no faction in the module has.*

### `J.22 The Driven Ground` — Where the Wedge Came Down, Nine Hundred Years On, Their Side of It
*Working note: the Monument of the Driving Down at `GA` from underneath, and it does not read the same way from here.*

### `J.23 The Sunless Water` — Below Everything, Still, Feeds the Seeps
*Working note: the bottom of the water system, and `FB.3`'s tanks come from here.*

### `J.24 Further Down` — It Keeps Going, Nobody Has Been, The Module Does Not Say
*Working note: the floor is not the floor. Left deliberately unwritten. **A mythic underworld needs somewhere it has not finished going**, and this is the module declining, on purpose, to answer one question.*
