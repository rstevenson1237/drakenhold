# `HD Zarkel, Peak 3 Level 3 Temple of Stone`

**Classification:** DANGEROUS · **Difficulty Die:** d6 · **Tags:** The Initiate's Circuit, Observation Rooms, Skybridge Terminus

*Region file. Companion to the Drakenhold Setting Outline and the Setting Relational Diagram. Tables are authored at the location-stub pass (procedure step 7); location stubs, outlines and the region relational diagram follow in the engineer phase.*

---


**Overview:** A circuit that still runs as designed, and a shortcut that costs more than the road. A main receiving room opens onto a circular route of trials through which initiates proved themselves worthy, and a party can undertake the initiate's ordeal honestly and be granted a Runemaster rod at its completion. The central area holds the secret rooms from which the Runemaster leadership conducted and observed the trials; those rooms permit cheating the circuit entirely and are the most heavily gated space on the level. Scattered notes here begin to name the artifact for what it was. HD is one of the three horizontal termini of the Skybridge.

**Ambiance:** Cut stone in a circuit, deliberately plain, deliberately disorienting. The receiving room is formal and the circuit beyond it is not. Sound behaves oddly on the route — footsteps arrive from the wrong direction, which is engineered rather than uncanny.

**Layout:** A main receiving room opening onto a circular route of trial chambers, with the observation rooms at the centre of the floor, gated separately and heavily. Around thirty rooms. HD is one of the three horizontal termini of the Skybridge.

**Features:** The circuit, which remains fully runnable as designed — a party can undertake the initiate's ordeal honestly and be granted a Runemaster rod at its completion. The observation rooms, from which the leadership conducted and watched the trials, which permit cheating the circuit entirely and are the most heavily gated space on the level. The shortcut costs more than the road, which is the level's entire argument.

**Dangers:** The trials, which are real, calibrated for dwarven initiates and not calibrated down for anyone else. Failing one has consequences the circuit was designed to impose.

**Creatures:** Constructs holding trial posts. Nothing hostile that was not placed here on purpose.

**Secrets:** Scattered notes here begin to name the artifact for what it was. The observation rooms hold assessments of individual initiates, including one who was failed for the wrong reasons — that assessment is Wyla Fenn's, it is worthless to anyone who does not know what it is, and it is the price of the only living account of Peak 3 (see A.13). A route from the observation rooms to HE that does not use the ramp.

**Treasure:** A Runemaster rod, earned. The observation rooms hold the leadership's own equipment.

**Reading the diagram.** Solid edges are open floor and open circuit. Dotted edges are gated, secret or not yet through: the tile at the Skybridge terminus, the two ways into the centre, the excavation arriving from below, and the way up into `HE`. The circuit is drawn as a literal ring because it is one.

**What the drawing found.**

- **The circuit is a closed ring with the grant on it, and the ring is the lock.** `HD.4`→`HD.5`→`HD.6`→`HD.7`→`HD.8`→`HD.9`→`HD.10`→`HD.4`. There is no chord across it and no room hangs off it. The grant sits one edge from the entry going backwards and six edges from it going forwards, and **the route imposes an attempt at every segment either way** — so walking the ring anticlockwise to collect the rod without running the trials means running the same five trials in reverse order. The shortest path to `HD.10` is one edge long and it costs exactly as much as the long one. That is a better lock than a door, and it does not need a single house rule.
- **The centre has two doors and they are the level's whole argument stated as topology.** `HD.4`–`HD.11` is the rod. `HD.10`–`HD.11` is honest completion. Both land in the same room, and the room holds everything on the floor worth carrying: the notes, the assessment, the sixth piece, the leadership's rods and the way up. **A party that cheats the circuit needs a Runemaster rod to do it, and the only Runemaster rod it can legitimately hold is the one at the far end of the circuit.** The shortcut costs more than the road because the shortcut is priced in the road.
- **Every leaf on this level is inside the gate.** `HD.12` through `HD.16` all hang off `HD.11` and off nothing else. Five of the level's six most consequential locations are behind one door, which is the sharpest concentration of reward-behind-a-single-condition anywhere in the module. It is deliberate and it is stated by the Runemasters themselves: this is how much they trusted their own initiates.
- **The excavation arrives on the ring, not in the centre.** `HC.12`–`HD.4`. It is a hole into the plain circuit ground, which means the clock running downstairs gets a party — or a kobold, or whatever the trials were containing — onto the trial route without the receiving room, without the terms stated, and without `HC.15`'s briefing. **It shortcuts the explanation, not the ordeal.** Nothing about the dig touches `HD.11`, so the excavation cannot deliver the observation rooms to anybody, and the level's argument survives its own live edge.
- **`HD.3` is a leaf on the receiving room and it faces outward.** The Skybridge's eastern terminus opens directly onto the level's one formal, honest, fully-explained room. A party arriving over the span from `I` lands on Peak 3 at exactly the point the Runemasters intended visitors to arrive at, having skipped every other level of the peak, and nobody has been watching this end for thirty years.
- **`HD.11` has a third door and it was never cut.** *Ratified at the close of step 8.* `HC.11`'s survey line was laid to arrive here — the leadership's own stair up from the chapel, bypassing the receiving room, the terms and the circuit. The order drew itself a private way into the room it watched its initiates from and then did not build it. It is **not** an edge, because it does not exist; what exists is a surveyed line one level down that a party can put a kobold work gang onto. **Redirecting the excavation onto the true line is the only thing in the module that would hand `HD.11` to somebody who did not pay for it**, and the level's argument survives only because Vekkut cannot read the survey he is following.
- **`HD.16` is the only edge out of the top of this level that is not the ramp**, and it leaves from inside the gate. The most heavily gated space on the peak opens onto the least safe one, and `HE.3` is the stable end of it — which makes the observation rooms a party's line of retreat out of the Sanctuary as well as their way into it.

**Route ends recorded.** Five edges leave region `HD`. `HC.1`–`HD.1` and `HD.1`–`HE.1` are the great ramp, drawn at both ends. `HC.12`–`HD.4` is the excavation and `HD.16`–`HE.3` the way up, both drawn at both ends. `HD.3`–`I.4` is the Skybridge's eastern terminus, drawn from this side; `I` reciprocates at its own pass.
