# `A Thornhaven`

**Classification:** SAFE · **Difficulty Die:** d6 · **Tags:** Faded Trade Town, River Landing, Last Safe Bed

*Region file. Companion to the Drakenhold Setting Outline and the Setting Relational Diagram. Tables are authored at the location-stub pass (procedure step 7); location stubs, outlines and the region relational diagram follow in the engineer phase.*

---


**Overview:** The last safe bed on the road and the only SAFE region in the module. Thornhaven is a stockaded river landing raised on and partly built out of salvaged Drakenhold stone, and everything about it is smaller than it was — the upper town holds the well, the old toll-house and what is left of the trade houses, while the wharf quarter below has gone to transients and warehouses idle since the road closed a generation ago. Its job is resupply, rumour, downtime and the module's first lesson: the haunted farmstead outside town is a two-session dungeon that teaches every rule the hold will later enforce, and it teaches them at survivable scale. Common provisions, rope, oil and porters are readily had; anything skilled, specialist or dwarven must be sent downriver for, which is a clock the party will feel later. The town has two live problems of its own — the witch at the wood's edge and the graves under the farmstead — and both are winnable by negotiation rather than force, which is the register the whole region plays in. The d6 is small-town scale, not danger.

**Ambiance:** Wet timber and river mud, woodsmoke, tar. The palisade is new work in old stone — dwarven blocks with the runes still on them, laid by people who could not read them and did not care, some set upside down. The upper town is swept and quiet and too large for the number of people in it; the wharf quarter below smells of rope, fish and spilled beer, and half its warehouses have doors that have not been opened since the road closed. Saplings have come up through the boards of the shuttered trade houses.

**Layout:** A stockaded landing on the east bank, built up a shallow slope in two tiers. The upper town gathers around the well and the old toll-house — reeve's hall, temple, two inns, the shuttered trade houses. The wharf quarter runs along the water below it, warehouses and drinking-halls and the boat landing. The road out runs north-west toward the Ironwood; the river runs south to civilisation. Wyla Fenn's grove sits an hour's walk out along the wood's edge, and the haunted farmstead half that, inland. Small enough to cross on foot in a quarter hour.

**Features:** Commerce, at the availability tiers the region's poverty sets. Downtime in all its forms, and the only Rest that recovers everything. The notice board at the toll-house, which is where the reeve's problems become jobs. The palisade itself, which is an ongoing act of theft from the grove and the root of the town's live feud. The farmstead, a small dungeon of a cellar and the graveyard under it, which teaches names, grave-goods, respect for the dead and the consequence of taking what is not offered — every rule the hold will later enforce, at survivable scale.

**Dangers:** None standing. The town does not answer intrusion; it gossips about it. Its two hazards are consequences rather than threats — the grove answers when its boundary is crossed, and the farmstead's dead rise nightly and strike at the neighbours once the relics are disturbed and until they are laid to rest.

**Creatures:** People, mostly. Reeve Aldis Marrow, who wants the farmstead settled before the outlying families abandon their fields. Ganner Skeed at the wharf, who knows every boat that has come up the river in twenty years. Wyla Fenn at the wood's edge, Runemaster-trained and living as a hedge-witch because she has nowhere else to be — the feud over the palisade timber is winnable by anyone willing to negotiate over trees, and the reward is the only living voice on what happened inside Peak 3. The Grellick Charter reprovisions here openly. Ashen's Crew are known and unwelcome. The farmstead's dead are the town's own refugees from the year after the descent.

**Secrets:** That the graves under the farmstead are Drakenhold refugees and the relics are their grave-goods, which is discoverable from the stones themselves if anyone reads the older script. That Wyla Fenn was inside Peak 3 and left before the Breaking finished. That several of the older families in the upper town are descended from dwarven trade partners and hold rods, tiles or ledger fragments in attics as heirlooms nobody has looked at in thirty years.

**Treasure:** Grave-goods from the farmstead — dwarven work, modest in coin and enormous in information. Whatever the upper town's attics turn up for a party that thinks to ask. Nothing else here is worth carrying.

## LOCATION STUBS

*Procedure step 7. Code, name and three thematic tags per location. The working note under each is scaffolding for step 8 and is absorbed into the two Overviews when they are written — it is not final text.*

**Upper town**

### `A.1 The Landing Gate` — Stolen Stone, Runes Upside Down, First Impression
*Working note: the module's thesis in one wall. Dwarven blocks with the inscription still on them, set by people who could not read them. A party that stops here reads its first runic script before anyone tells them it matters.*

### `A.2 The Well Square` — Swept and Too Large, Public Ground, Where Talk Starts
*Working note: the upper town gathers here and there are not enough people to fill it. Gather Information's natural home.*

### `A.3 The Old Toll-House` — The Notice Board, Ledgers Nobody Reads, Closed Road
*Working note: the reeve's problems become jobs here. The toll ledgers record thirty years of traffic thinning to nothing, and the last dwarven entries are in two scripts.*

### `A.4 The Reeve's Hall` — Aldis Marrow, Outlying Families, A Problem He Cannot Solve
*Working note: the farmstead job comes from here. Marrow wants it settled before the outlying families abandon their fields, and he will pay in coin, standing and a name or two.*

### `A.5 The Riverside Temple` — Underattended, Rites for the Drowned, Quiet Charity
*Working note: the only place in town that will take a body without asking questions. Its practice around the dead is a working model of what the hold's dead expect and nobody makes that connection out loud.*

### `A.6 The Kingfisher` — Upper-Town Inn, Respectable, Where the Charter Drinks
*Working note: the Grellick Charter reprovisions and lodges here openly, in good order, paying well. Rest, and the only Rest in the module that recovers everything.*

### `A.7 The Shuttered Trade Houses` — Saplings Through the Boards, Locked Attics, Dwarven Partners
*Working note: several of the older families are descended from the hold's trade partners and hold rods, tiles or ledger fragments in attics nobody has opened in thirty years. Obtainable by asking, by buying, or by being the sort of people who are told.*

**Wharf quarter**

### `A.8 The Boat Landing` — The Way Downriver, Specialist Goods, A Clock
*Working note: anything skilled, specialist or dwarven must be sent downriver for. The party will feel this as a delay later and should meet it as a fact now.*

### `A.9 Skeed's Wharf Office` — Twenty Years of Manifests, Ganner Skeed, Who Came and Who Did Not
*Working note: Skeed knows every boat that has come up the river in twenty years and every crew that walked up the road. He is the register of who has gone into Drakenhold and not come out.*

### `A.10 The Idle Warehouses` — Doors Unopened Since the Road Closed, Rot and Rope, Squatters
*Working note: transients, one or two things worth finding, and the physical fact of a trade economy that stopped mid-sentence.*

### `A.11 The Sunken Oar` — Drinking Hall, Rough Custom, Ashen's Crew Are Known Here
*Working note: Carousing. Ashen's Crew are known and unwelcome, which does not stop them. Rumours run rougher and less true down here than in the square.*

**Outskirts**

### `A.12 The Cutting Ground` — Fresh Stumps, The Live Feud, Timber for the Palisade
*Working note: the town takes its palisade timber from the wood's edge and has been warned. The feud is winnable by anyone willing to negotiate over trees.*

### `A.13 Wyla Fenn's Grove` — Runemaster-Trained, A Boundary Marked Plainly, The Only Living Witness
*Working note: she was inside Peak 3 and left before the Breaking finished. She is the only living voice on what happened up there, and she gives nothing to anyone who arrives as a customer.*

*Her price is a personal one and it is not treasure. She wants back the written assessment of the trial she was failed in — held in HD's observation rooms, the initiate failed for the wrong reasons. It is a plaque of no value whatever to anyone who does not already know what it is, and a party that has not spoken with her will walk past it. She will not describe it in enough detail to be found by a party that has not earned the description. Cross-referenced into HD at its location pass.*

**Ketter's Farmstead**

### `A.14 The Farmyard` — Abandoned Mid-Season, Neighbours Watching From Afar, Nightly Visitors
*Working note: the approach, the evidence, and the decision to go in. The dead rise nightly and strike at the neighbours only once the relics are disturbed — a party that arrives before the disturbance sees a different problem than one that arrives after.*

### `A.15 The Farmhouse` — Interrupted Household, The Family That Dug, What Is Gone For Good
*Working note: the Ketters found the graves, took the grave-goods and sold the best of them downriver. The record of what left is here, and what left is not coming back — the buyer is three months gone and the goods are in a city nobody in this module visits. The dead can still be laid to rest without them, and the party learns that some losses are simply permanent.*

### `A.16 The Cellar Stair` — Cut Too Wide, Older Than the House, First Dwarven Cutting
*Working note: the house was built on top of something. The stair is the tell and it is visible to anyone who looks at the stonework.*

### `A.17 The Cold Store` — Provisions Gone Over, The Breach in the Wall, Cold Air From Below
*Working note: the Ketters broke through a wall that was not theirs, and the breach is how everything since has happened.*

### `A.18 The First Gallery` — Refugee Interment, Names in Two Scripts, Grave-Goods Disturbed
*Working note: these are Drakenhold refugees who died the year after the descent, buried by their own. The older script on the stones is the same script as the waystones. This is where a party learns that names on stones are worth reading.*

### `A.19 The Lower Niches` — Deeper Cutting, Undisturbed, What the Ketters Did Not Reach
*Working note: the negative-space room. Intact, respectful, and worth nothing to a looter — which is the lesson, delivered by withholding.*

### `A.20 The Grave of Brannek Kelmor` — The Name That Settles It, Laid With Care, The First Key
*Working note: **Brannek Kelmor** — bran and mek, store and work, a name that was a job before it was a person. He led the refugees out and they buried him properly with what they still had. The dead are laid to rest by returning what remains of what was taken and speaking his name at the stone. Force works badly and is meant to.*

*His name has two further lives. It is cut on the black obelisk in HA, minor and easily missed, and a party that reads it there thirty sessions later recognises the man they buried. And it opens one thing in FA — a stores tally-niche in the dry-goods warren of the servants' passages, his own, holding nothing of value and proving the rule: names are keys. Cross-referenced into HA and FA at their location passes.*

## TABLES

**Events** — d6, rolled on entry and at the start of each week the party stays. Ascending.

| d6 | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | **The Farmstead Answers.** A body comes down the road from the outlying fields, or a family arrives in the square with what is left of one. Marrow's price for the job goes up and so does the town's patience running out. |
| 2 | **The Grove Is Crossed.** Another cart of timber comes in off the wood's edge. Wyla Fenn will not speak to anyone from Thornhaven this week, and someone in the cutting crew is missing. |
| 3 | **Ashen's Crew Are In Town.** Seven of them, buying rope, oil and silence at the Sunken Oar, and asking the same questions the party is. They leave before dawn and they are ahead. |
| 4 | **The Charter Reprovisions.** The Grellick Charter comes down for mules, salt and hands. Hiring is easy this week, prices are up, and the surveyor will talk to anyone who buys the second round. |
| 5 | **A Boat From Downriver.** Specialist goods, news, and a buyer who pays properly for dwarven work. One outstanding order arrives. |
| 6 | **Someone Comes Back.** A survivor walks out of the Ironwood — half a crew, or one of it — carrying nothing and saying little. What they say is true, partial, and about a region the party has not reached yet. |
