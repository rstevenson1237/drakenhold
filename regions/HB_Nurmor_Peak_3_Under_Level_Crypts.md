# `HB Nurmor, Peak 3 Under Level Crypts`

**Classification:** DANGEROUS · **Difficulty Die:** d6 · **Tags:** Tiered by Station, Haunted Galleries, A Niche That Should Not Exist

*Region file. Companion to the Drakenhold Setting Outline and the Setting Relational Diagram. Tables are authored at the location-stub pass (procedure step 7); location stubs, outlines and the region relational diagram follow in the engineer phase.*

---


**Overview:** Interment strictly tiered by station — royal, Runemaster, guild and guard, each in its own descending gallery, and the underclass given no crypt at all. That omission is the region. It is why the last survivors bury their own in the servants' passages, and why a concealed niche cut into a wall that should have been blank has gone thirty years unnoticed. Torvin Ganthur was laid there by the last dwarves who could reach him, and the lance was laid with him. Finding the niche requires knowing his name, and his name exists nowhere in this level's official carving. Everything else here is trapped ancestral wealth and things that resent being disturbed for it.

**Ambiance:** Dry, cold, and stacked. Galleries descend by station, and the quality of the cutting falls with each one. Royal above, then Runemaster, then guild, then guard. Below the guard gallery the rock is simply rock, because the underclass were given no crypt at all.

**Layout:** Four descending galleries, tiered strictly by station, each with its own approach and its own gating. The stairwell up to HA. Around forty rooms. The lowest wall — the one that should have been blank — is where the region turns.

**Features:** The tiering itself, which is the region: interment by station, and the omission at the bottom of it. That omission is why the last survivors bury their own in the servants' passages, and why a concealed niche cut into a wall that should have been blank has gone thirty years unnoticed. Torvin Ganthur was laid there by the last dwarves who could reach him, and the lance was laid with him. Finding the niche requires knowing his name, and his name exists nowhere in this level's official carving.

**Dangers:** Ancestral wealth, trapped by people who expected grave-robbers and were right. Things in the galleries that resent being disturbed for it. The royal gallery is the worst of both.

**Creatures:** Undead through the galleries, thickening downward, in variety. Nothing that came here recently. *Answered at step 8, from the existing bestiary and with no new template proposed: **Dwarven Wraiths** on the royal and Runemaster tiers, who may be addressed as people by anyone arriving with a name; **Skeletal Warriors** in the guild and guard galleries, still holding post; **Shadows** below the tiers, where no crypt was cut and no light ever was either. The variety is tiered like everything else on the level, which is the region's own argument, and the wraiths are why a party collecting royal names upstairs is collecting a weapon.*

**Secrets:** The niche, and what is in it. Which royal interments are empty and why. That the guard gallery holds several dwarves who died after the descent rather than during it, which means somebody was still burying properly for longer than anyone thinks.

**Treasure:** Grave-goods, tiered like everything else — and the lance, which is not treasure so much as a question.

**Reading the diagram.** Solid edges are open floor. `HB.9` and `HB.18` are distributed conditions rather than rooms — the grave-goods and the dead that object to them are drawn touching each of the four galleries, because that is exactly how they are placed. Dotted edges are hidden or secret: the two ways in from `HA` that are not the stair, the niche, and the descent into `J`.

**What the drawing found.**

- **The tiering is a chain and the level is a funnel.** `HB.2`→`HB.4`→`HB.7`→`HB.8`→`HB.11`→`HB.14`. A party entering by the stairwell reaches the bottom of the crypts — which is where the niche, the artifact piece and the way into `J` all are — only by crossing all four stations of the dead in descending order, richest and worst first. **That is a single throat, and the drawing found two answers to it, both of them already promised.**
- **The two answers are `HA`'s, and they are priced in the one currency this region deals in.** `HA.29`–`HB.3` lands in `HB.12`, the late burials, in the guard gallery — three tiers below the royal ground — and it is warded from above by people who are old, which is why the ward is the module's loaded trigger rather than a lock. `HA.21`–`HB.17` lands lower still, in the survivors' own graves, one edge from the blank wall. **The best route into the crypts runs through the burial ground of the people whose good opinion is Peak 3's largest faction asset**, and nothing about it is stealthy. The price of the shortest way to the niche is that the pocket knows you walked over their dead to reach it.
- **`HB.13` is the only edge on the level that crosses a station, and somebody with authority cut it once.** The watch that stayed were interred with their post rather than their station, which puts a guard-gallery burial on royal ground and makes `HB.4`–`HB.13`–`HB.11` a route that steps three tiers in one cutting. It is also the one place where `HB.18`'s rule — *they do not pursue past their own tier* — has an exception built into the architecture, and the exception is discoverable by reading dates rather than by fighting anything.
- **`HB.18` turns the region from a grind into a geography problem, and the diagram is what makes that legible.** Four galleries, four sets of dead, none of them following past their own edge. A party that works this out crosses the level in stages with rests between them. A party that does not is fighting the whole floor at once for no reason.
- **The niche is not off the blank wall. It is off the older course.** `HB.14`–`HB.20`–`HB.15`, and only the last of those is secret. The wall the underclass were given instead of a crypt is where the founding lineage's plainer cutting surfaces, and the survivors cut Torvin Ganthur's niche into it without ever having been taught that manner. **The discovery is therefore two steps and only one of them is the name** — notice the older course in unworked stone, then know a man who is on no wall on this floor.
- **`HB.10` is at the head of the tiering, not buried in it.** The interment record is two edges from the stairwell foot, so the level hands a party its own index before it hands them anything else. Everything the record does not contain is available to be noticed from the first ten minutes, and the module never points at the omission.
- **`HB.5`–`HB.6` is a leaf pair on royal ground and that is correct.** The empty interments and the interment that was never filled are on the way to nothing. Reading them is always a choice made in the worst room on the level.

**Route ends recorded.** Four edges leave region `HB`. `HA.10`–`HB.1` is the stairwell, `HA.29`–`HB.3` the warded service way and `HA.21`–`HB.17` the survivors' burials — all three drawn at the first-level block's pass and **confirmed here exactly as `HA` drew them**. `HB.19`–`J.2` is Peak 3's declared descent, drawn from this side; `J` reciprocates at its own pass.
