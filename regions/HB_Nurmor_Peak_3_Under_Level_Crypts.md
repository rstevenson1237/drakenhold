# `HB Nurmor, Peak 3 Under Level Crypts`

**Classification:** DANGEROUS · **Difficulty Die:** d6 · **Tags:** Tiered by Station, Haunted Galleries, A Niche That Should Not Exist

*Region file. Companion to the Drakenhold Setting Outline and the Setting Relational Diagram. Tables are authored at the location-stub pass (procedure step 7); location stubs, outlines and the region relational diagram follow in the engineer phase.*

---


**Overview:** Interment strictly tiered by station — royal, Runemaster, guild and guard, each in its own descending gallery, and the underclass given no crypt at all. That omission is the region. It is why the last survivors bury their own in the servants' passages, and why a concealed niche cut into a wall that should have been blank has gone thirty years unnoticed. Torvin Ganthur was laid there by the last dwarves who could reach him, and the lance was laid with him. Finding the niche requires knowing his name, and his name exists nowhere in this level's official carving. Everything else here is trapped ancestral wealth and things that resent being disturbed for it.

**Ambiance:** Dry, cold, and stacked. Galleries descend by station, and the quality of the cutting falls with each one. Royal above, then Runemaster, then guild, then guard. Below the guard gallery the rock is simply rock, because the underclass were given no crypt at all.

**Layout:** Four descending galleries, tiered strictly by station, each with its own approach and its own gating. The stairwell up to HA. Around forty rooms. The lowest wall — the one that should have been blank — is where the region turns.

**Features:** The tiering itself, which is the region: interment by station, and the omission at the bottom of it. That omission is why the last survivors bury their own in the servants' passages, and why a concealed niche cut into a wall that should have been blank has gone thirty years unnoticed. Torvin Ganthur was laid there by the last dwarves who could reach him, and the lance was laid with him. Finding the niche requires knowing his name, and his name exists nowhere in this level's official carving.

**Dangers:** Ancestral wealth, trapped by people who expected grave-robbers and were right. Things in the galleries that resent being disturbed for it. The royal gallery is the worst of both.

**Creatures:** Undead through the galleries, thickening downward, in variety. Nothing that came here recently.

**Secrets:** The niche, and what is in it. Which royal interments are empty and why. That the guard gallery holds several dwarves who died after the descent rather than during it, which means somebody was still burying properly for longer than anyone thinks.

**Treasure:** Grave-goods, tiered like everything else — and the lance, which is not treasure so much as a question.

## LOCATION STUBS

*Procedure step 7. Code, name and three thematic tags per location. Block-level material — the clue chain, the niche, the excavation and the room budget — lives in `PEAK_3_BLOCK.md`.*

*40 rooms budgeted; **20 are stubbed as locations** and the balance is unnamed fill — niches by the score, gallery runs, ante-chambers, the ordinary repetition of a place built to hold nine hundred years of dead in order.*

**The descent**

### `HB.1 The Stairwell Foot` — Black Stone, Dry Cold, Ordered
*Working note: the crypts announce themselves as architecture rather than as horror. Everything is in its place and the place is the point.*

### `HB.2 The Tiering` — Four Galleries Descending, Quality Falling With Each, Deliberate
*Working note: royal, then Runemaster, then guild, then guard, in that order, downward. The cutting degrades with station and the degradation is legible at a glance. **This is the region** — not a layout note but its whole argument.*

### `HB.3 The Crypt Access` — Service Way From Above, Warded Homemade, Cuts Both Ways
*Working note: the far end of `HA.29`. Shut and warded badly and adequately, and the ward is degrading because the people who maintain it are old. A loaded trigger fired by action or inaction, and this is the room it fires from.*

**The royal gallery**

### `HB.4 The Royal Gallery` — Nine Hundred Years of Kings, Best Cutting in the Hold, Worst Ground On It
*Working note: the richest and most dangerous tier, and a party walks into it without solving anything. Getting out is the problem.*

### `HB.5 The Empty Interments` — Sealed, Named, Nobody Inside
*Working note: several royal interments contain no king. Some are honest — bodies never recovered from campaigns. At least one is not, and the difference is legible to anyone who reads the seals rather than the names.*

### `HB.6 Baldrun Azkelith's Place` — Prepared, Waiting, Never Filled
*Working note: his interment was cut and readied and he never arrived in it. Whatever happened to the last king, it did not end here, and `GC` will not say either.*

**The Runemaster and guild galleries**

### `HB.7 The Runemaster Gallery` — Sealed Differently, Rod-Gated, Fewer Than Expected
*Working note: their own tier, gated in their own manner, and thinner than the order's size accounts for. The Runemasters were not all buried here and the gallery does not explain where the rest went.*

### `HB.8 The Guild Gallery` — Six Houses in Ranks, Kelmor Among Them, Wealth by the Yard
*Working note: the most straightforwardly lucrative tier. A party that buried Brannek Kelmor at Thornhaven finds his house here and the recognition is the module paying back a small kindness a long way downstream.*

### `HB.9 The Grave-Goods` — Tiered Like Everything Else, Trapped by Professionals, Worth Taking
*Working note: distributed rather than roomed. Real wealth, real traps, and a moral cost the module states and does not adjudicate.*

### `HB.10 The Interment Record` — Who Lies Where, Complete to the Descent, Official
*Working note: the level's index, and the crucial fact about it is what it does not contain. Nobody without station appears. **Torvin Ganthur is not in it.***

**The guard gallery**

### `HB.11 The Guard Gallery` — Soldiers in Rank Again, Plainer, Some Died Later
*Working note: several here died *after* the descent rather than during it, which means somebody was still burying properly for years afterward. The dates are cut and they are readable.*

### `HB.12 The Late Burials` — Cut Rough, Cut Right, By Hands That Were Not Masons
*Working note: the last decade of proper burials, done badly and done with care, by the survivors before they gave up on reaching this floor. The names are people the pocket upstairs still remembers.*

### `HB.13 The Watch That Stayed` — Guards Interred With Their Post, Not Their Station, Somebody Chose This
*Working note: a break in the tiering, and the only one on the level. Whoever made it had authority and used it once.*

**Below the tiers**

### `HB.14 The Blank Wall` — Where the Rock Is Simply Rock, No Crypt at All, The Omission
*Working note: the underclass were given nothing. The wall below the guard gallery is unworked stone and it is the largest single statement the hold makes about itself. **Artifact piece five is behind it**, sealed into an empty royal interment moved down here rather than left where kings could be robbed. Whoever did it understood that the safest place in a hold that tiers everything by station is the one tier it refuses to acknowledge.*

### `HB.15 The Niche` — Cut Into a Wall That Should Be Blank, Thirty Years Unnoticed, Opened by a Name
*Working note: Torvin Ganthur, laid here by the last dwarves who could reach him, and the lance laid with him. **His name opens it and his name is carved nowhere on this level** — not in the record, not on the obelisk at `HA.5`. It is at Thornhaven, under the benches at `GB.9`, and in the mouths of people still alive upstairs.*

### `HB.16 The Lance` — Laid as Honour, Not as Armament, Nobody Left It Ready
*Working note: it is not treasure so much as a question. Nothing about how it was laid suggests it was meant to be used again, which is the first thing a party should notice and the last thing they will believe.*

### `HB.17 The Survivors' Burials` — In the Passages, Not the Galleries, Recent
*Working note: the pocket buries its own in the servants' warren because the floor below the guard gallery holds nothing and they will not lay their people in rock. Reachable from `HA.29`, and disturbing them is the fastest way to lose Peak 3's largest survivor group.*

### `HB.18 The Thickening` — Undead Increasing Downward, By Tier, Never Past Their Own
*Working note: they do not pursue past their station's gallery. That is a rule of the level, it is discoverable in one careful crossing, and it turns the whole region from a grind into a geography problem.*

### `HB.19 The Marked Descent` — Peak 3's Declared Way Down, Cut and Signed, Air Coming Up
*Working note: the honest route into `J` from this peak, below everything.*

### `HB.20 The Older Course` — Plainer Stone, No Clan Mark, Running Downward
*Working note: as `FA.34`, `GA.25`, `HA.28` and `GB.16`. Here it runs down, and the wall the niche is cut into is part of it — which means the founding lineage's manner is the manner the survivors instinctively cut in, nine hundred years after nobody was taught it.*
