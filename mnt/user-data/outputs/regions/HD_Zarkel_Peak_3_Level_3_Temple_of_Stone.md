# `HD Zarkel, Peak 3 Level 3 Temple of Stone`

**Classification:** DANGEROUS · **Difficulty Die:** d6 · **Tags:** The Initiate's Circuit, Observation Rooms, Skybridge Terminus

*Region file. Companion to the Drakenhold Setting Outline and the Setting Relational Diagram. Tables are authored at the location-stub pass (procedure step 7); location stubs, outlines and the region relational diagram follow in the engineer phase.*

---


**Overview:** A circuit that still runs as designed, and a shortcut that costs more than the road. A main receiving room opens onto a circular route of trials through which initiates proved themselves worthy, and a party can undertake the initiate's ordeal honestly and be granted a Runemaster rod at its completion. The central area holds the secret rooms from which the Runemaster leadership conducted and observed the trials; those rooms permit cheating the circuit entirely and are the most heavily gated space on the level. Scattered notes here begin to name the artifact for what it was. HD is one of the three horizontal termini of the Skybridge.

**Ambiance:** Cut stone in a circuit, deliberately plain, deliberately disorienting. The receiving room is formal and the circuit beyond it is not. Sound behaves oddly on the route — footsteps arrive from the wrong direction, which is engineered rather than uncanny.

**Layout:** A main receiving room opening onto a circular route of trial chambers, with the observation rooms at the centre of the floor, gated separately and heavily. Around thirty rooms. HD is one of the three horizontal termini of the Skybridge.

**Features:** The circuit, which remains fully runnable as designed — a party can undertake the initiate's ordeal honestly and be granted a Runemaster rod at its completion. The observation rooms, from which the leadership conducted and watched the trials, which permit cheating the circuit entirely and are the most heavily gated space on the level. The shortcut costs more than the road, which is the level's entire argument.

**Dangers:** The trials, which are real, calibrated for dwarven initiates and not calibrated down for anyone else. Failing one has consequences the circuit was designed to impose.

**Creatures:** Constructs holding trial posts. Nothing hostile that was not placed here on purpose.

**Secrets:** Scattered notes here begin to name the artifact for what it was. The observation rooms hold assessments of individual initiates, including one who was failed for the wrong reasons — that assessment is Wyla Fenn's, it is worthless to anyone who does not know what it is, and it is the price of the only living account of Peak 3 (see A.13). A route from the observation rooms to HE that does not use the ramp.

**Treasure:** A Runemaster rod, earned. The observation rooms hold the leadership's own equipment.

## LOCATION STUBS

*Procedure step 7. Block-level material lives in `PEAK_3_BLOCK.md`.*

*30 rooms budgeted; **16 are stubbed as locations** and the balance is unnamed fill — plain circuit ground, deliberately featureless, deliberately identical, and the featurelessness is the trial rather than an absence of one.*

**The receiving floor**

### `HD.1 The Ramp Head` — Formal, Announced, The Last Plain Statement
*Working note: the level tells a party exactly what it is before it starts being difficult. Everything after this room is engineered to be harder than it looks.*

### `HD.2 The Receiving Room` — Formal, Cut Well, Where the Circuit Is Explained
*Working note: the terms of the ordeal, stated in full, in runic script, without deception. The Runemasters did not cheat their initiates. They simply did not lower the bar.*

### `HD.3 The Skybridge Terminus` — Tile-Gated, Eastern End, Nobody Has Used It in Thirty Years
*Working note: the third of the Skybridge's three horizontal termini, and the only one that is neither checkpointed nor ruined. Peak 3 is reachable directly from `I` by anyone holding the tile.*

**The circuit**

### `HD.4 The Circuit` — Circular, Plain, Disorienting on Purpose
*Working note: the trial route itself, running as designed. The sameness is engineered and so is the sound. It is fully runnable and it does not require a single house rule to run.*

### `HD.5 The Trial of Weight` — Endurance, Measured, No Trick In It
*Working note: the first post, and it is honest. A party that expects a puzzle wastes an hour looking for one.*

### `HD.6 The Trial of the Word` — Script, Under Pressure, Read or Fail
*Working note: **the waystone lesson coming due.** Everything a party needs was taught on the road in `B` and confirmed at `HC.15`. This is the module's single clearest reward for having paid attention two hundred miles ago.*

### `HD.7 The Trial of the Dark` — No Light Permitted, Route by Touch, The Stone Is the Instruction
*Working note: cut guidance readable by hand, and the light rule from the deep stated in miniature and survivably.*

### `HD.8 The Trial of the Post` — A Construct, Holding, Not Trying to Kill Anyone
*Working note: the combat trial, and it is a bout rather than a fight. It stops when the standard is met and it will not pursue.*

### `HD.9 The Trial of Refusal` — What the Initiate Will Not Do, Recorded, Not Scored
*Working note: the last post, and it is the one nobody expects. It asks a question, records the answer, and passes everyone. **The observation rooms scored it anyway**, which is where Wyla Fenn's assessment comes from.*

### `HD.10 The Grant` — A Runemaster Rod, Earned, At the End of the Road
*Working note: **the highest-tier key in the module a party can hold legitimately.** It opens Runemaster work throughout the hold, including the gallery at `HB.7`, the sealed chamber at `HC.18` and the ward at `GD.9`, and nobody will ever be able to say it was stolen.*

**The centre**

### `HD.11 The Observation Rooms` — Secret, Heavily Gated, They Watched Everything
*Working note: the leadership's own space, at the centre of the floor, gated harder than anything else on the level. Reachable by rod or by running the circuit honestly, and **the fact that honest completion opens it is the level's whole argument** — the shortcut costs more than the road.*

### `HD.12 The Scattered Notes` — The Artifact, Beginning to Be Named, By People Only Starting to Suspect
*Working note: the third rung of the clue chain. Not a revelation — a set of careful, worried, incomplete observations by senior members who were working out what they had and were being careful about writing it down.*

### `HD.13 Wyla Fenn's Assessment` — Failed for the Wrong Reasons, Filed, Worthless to Anyone Else
*Working note: **the price at `A.13`.** She was failed by the observers rather than by the circuit, on `HD.9`, for an answer that was correct. It is a single sheet, it means nothing to anybody who does not know what it is, and it buys the only living account of Peak 3.*

### `HD.14 The Hidden Piece` — Put Here by Somebody Who Did Not Get Back Out, Behind the Leadership's Own Gate
*Working note: **artifact piece six.** Carried down from the Sanctuary in the season of the Breaking and hidden in the most heavily gated room on the peak by a Runemaster who then went back up and is named on the register at `HE.10` as one of the three entries that trails off.*

### `HD.15 The Leadership's Equipment` — Their Own Rods, Their Own Work, Left Where It Was Used
*Working note: the observation rooms' material treasure, and it is considerable. Rods of a tier a party will not otherwise see below `HE`.*

### `HD.16 The Way Up` — From the Observation Rooms, Not the Ramp, Secret
*Working note: the route into `HE` that does not use the great ramp. The most heavily gated space on the level opens onto the least safe one, which is the order stating precisely how much it trusted its own initiates.*
