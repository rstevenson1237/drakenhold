## RUMOURS

*d20, rolled for the Gather Information move. Setting-wide, though the great majority concern Drakenhold. The Referee refreshes entries as the campaign consumes them.*

*Each entry carries a truth marker in the source, stripped by the renderer from anything player-facing: **`(T)`** true as stated, **`(P)`** partially true — something real, misremembered, exaggerated or attached to the wrong place — and **`(F)`** false. Expanding the false and partial sets is free. **Expanding the true set devalues every clue in the module**, because a party that finds rumour and fact reliably identical stops investigating and starts obeying.*

***Rumours 4, 7, 9 and 15 carry no marker, and the blank is deliberate.*** *Each rests on a question the module has not yet answered, and marking it would settle that question by side effect. They are open in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, each naming the pass that owns it. Until then the Referee holds them as genuinely undetermined, which is the honest state of them.*

1. (P) — The wyrm never came for gold. Something was buried in that mountain and he was sent for it, and whoever sent him is still waiting.
2. (T) — The great doors of the hold stand broken open. Nothing has come out of them in ten years, and that ought to frighten you more than if something had.
3. (P) — The King's name was scratched off every stone in Drakenhold, and he earned it — he sold the hold out from under his own people.
4. There is a lance in the mountain that killed a dragon once, and it will do it again for the right arm.
5. (P) — The Runemasters took something out of the earth that was never theirs, and it is still up there.
6. (T) — The goblins in the wood will talk to you. Bring them something dead and they will talk a long while.
7. The far guard booth at the river crossing is manned again, and they are charging.
8. (P) — He sleeps a year at a stretch and wakes for a year, and by the reckoning of it this is the sleeping year.
9. Kobolds hold the granaries under the first peak. They would rather trade than fight, which makes them the only reasonable thing in that mountain.
10. (F) — Every dwarf in Drakenhold is dead. Whatever you hear moving in there is wearing them.
11. (P) — The lizard-folk run the toll for the wyrm, and they skim it, and one day he will notice.
12. (F) — The wyrm is dying. That is why the sightings stopped. Walk up there now and you will find a carcass on a pile of gold.
13. (T) — The old gates want carved tiles, not keys. A cracked one is worth nothing — the stone has to be whole.
14. (P) — Ashen's crew found a way into the third peak. They have been up there a month and they have not come down.
15. The third peak was never touched. Whatever the wyrm did to the rest of it, he did not go there, and he does not go there now.
16. (P) — That woman at the wood's edge called the wyrm down. Ask her where she was thirty years ago and watch her face.
17. (F) — Speak the King's name in Drakenhold and every door in the mountain opens for you.
18. (P) — Outside the celebration hall stands a black stone with every honoured dead of the hold cut into it by name. Every one.
19. (P) — There is a road under that mountain that comes out the far side of the Broken Teeth, and the dwarves used it to trade with things that do not have a country.
20. (P) — The iron men in the forge never stopped working. Bring them iron and they will let you pass — they only ever wanted the metal.
